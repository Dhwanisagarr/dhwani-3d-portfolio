DAHLIA turns everyday photos into a quiet, visual record of the year — one memory per day, one growing garden.

## Problem

Camera rolls are full, but memories feel invisible. Journals add pressure.

## Solution

Built a visual memory garden where each photo grows a unique botanical memory.

## Highlights

- Pick a date, add one photo, watch a flower appear in your garden
- Calendar navigation across months and years with botanical identities
- Calm, private, minimal design stored locally without streaks or pressure
