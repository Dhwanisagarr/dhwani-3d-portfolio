StreakUp is a challenge-based habit tracker built around showing progress, not just checking boxes.

## Problem

Most habit trackers focus on individual tasks, but don't make longer-term progress feel meaningful.

## Solution

Turned habits into time-bound challenges (21, 30, 66, 90 days) with visible streaks and progress calendars.

## Highlights

- 21, 30, 66, 90-day time-bound challenges with flexible completion
- Streaks, progress calendars, notes, and challenge templates
- Lightweight, login-free, local-first daily habit experience
