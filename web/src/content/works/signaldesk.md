A Voice-of-Customer tool that turns messy customer feedback into prioritized product issues backed by evidence.

## Problem

Customer feedback is everywhere. Product insight isn't. Recurring problems drown in noise.

## Solution

Built a deterministic workflow linking raw quotes directly to prioritized issues with PII masking.

## Highlights

- Evidence-backed issue cards with raw customer quote verification
- Deterministic frequency, recency, and impact score calculations
- Exportable product roadmap & issue summaries for stakeholders
