### 2. DAHLIA — A Personal Memory Garden
- **Category:** Product & Design
- **Role:** Product + Design + Frontend
- **Timeline:** 2026
- **Tools:** Next.js, React, JavaScript, CSS, IndexedDB, Vercel
- **Metrics:** 2-Week MVP, Visual Memory Map
- **Live URL:** https://mydahlia.vercel.app/

**Tagline:** DAHLIA turns everyday photos into a quiet, visual record of the year — one memory per day, one growing garden.

**Problem:** Camera rolls are full, but memories feel invisible. Journals add pressure.

**Solution:** Built a visual memory garden where each photo grows a unique botanical memory.

**Highlights:**
- Pick a date, add one photo, watch a flower appear in your garden
- Calendar navigation across months and years with botanical identities
- Calm, private, minimal design stored locally without streaks or pressure
